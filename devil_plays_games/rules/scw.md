# SCW

Four player 2v2 wrestling card game. Fight over wrestlers with rock paper scissors. Then jump in the ring with 6 tag team matches.

**Players:** 4
**Components:** Star deck, Manager deck, Belt deck

---

## Overview

SCW (Super Championship Wrestling), is a game in two acts. In **the Draft**, players compete to draft wrestlers by throwing Rock, Paper, Scissors. In **the Show**, players are paired into tag teams and compete in a series of wrestling matches played as tricks. The twist: you don't know who your partner will be until the Draft is over.

---

## Components

### Star Deck

The Star deck contains all the wrestlers available for drafting. Each Star card has:

- **Name and art** — the wrestler's identity
- **Power** — a number from 1 to 7 representing overall strength
- **Style** — one of three fighting styles: Clean, Dirty, or Flashy
- **Effect** — a unique ability that triggers during a match


### Manager Deck

~N Manager cards.


### Belt Deck

~N Belt cards.


---

## Game Play

1. **Setup**
- Shuffle the Belt, Manager, and Star decks separately.
- Deal one Manager card face up to each player.
- Deal 6 Belts face down.
2. **The Draft** — Draft wrestlers by throwing Rock Paper Scissors.
3. **Partner Assignment** — Reveal a wrestler to determine tag teams.
4. **The Show** — Play 6 matches (tricks). Each match awards a Belt.
5. **Winner** — The team with more Belts wins.

---

## The Draft

The Draft is how players build their roster of 6 wrestlers.

### How It Works

1. Flip the top card of the Star deck face up.
2. Any player who wants the card throws Rock, Paper, or Scissors simultaneously with the other interested players.
3. Resolve the throw:
   - **No one throws:** Discard the card. Nobody wanted them.
   - **One player throws:** They take the card.
   - **Two players throw:** Standard RPS. Winner takes the card.
   - **Three players throw:** If exactly one player threw the symbol that beats what the other two threw, that player wins. In any other case, all tied/conflicting players throw again. Anyone may drop out or stay in on any rethrow.
   - **Four players throw:** You can only win by being the sole player who threw the symbol that beats what the other three threw. Otherwise, rethrow. Anyone may drop out or stay in.
4. If a rethrow results in everyone dropping out, discard the card.
5. Flip the next card and repeat.

### Draft Ends

The draft ends when three players have exactly 6 wrestlers. The remaining player must now fill out their roster. Flip over two cards at a time. The player must choose one of the two cards to add to their roster, and the other card is discarded. Repeat until the player has 6 wrestlers.

*If the deck runs out* at any time during the draft, shuffle the discard pile and deal wrestlers face up to the remaining players until everyone has 6 wrestlers. No more rock paper scissors, no more choice. Just deal and go.

---

## Partner Assignment

Once all players have their rosters, it's time to form tag teams.

1. All four players simultaneously select one wrestler from their hand and play it face down.
- You may not a pick "Wild Card" wrestler for this step. You must pick a wrestler with a fixed style (Clean, Dirty, or Flashy).
2. All players reveal simultaneously.
- Ignore wrestler effects and power levels during the reveal. Only style matters.
3. Resolve partnerships:
   - **Two players revealed the same style:** Those two players are partners. The other two are partners.
   - **Three players revealed the same style:** The player who revealed a different style picks their partner. The remaining two are partners.
   - **All four revealed the same style:** Return wrestlers to hands and try again.
4. If partnerships are not resolved after 6 attempts, the youngest player picks their partner.
5. After partnerships are resolved, all revealed wrestlers are returned to their players' hands.
6. The player who picked their partner leads the first trick.


---

## The Show

The Show consists of 6 matches. Each match is played as a trick.

### Match Structure

1. **Flip a Belt** — Turn over the top Belt card. This is the prize for winning this match.
2. **Lead wrestler** — One player plays a wrestler from their hand face up into the ring.
3. **Call out** — The player who just played calls on any other player who hasn't played yet this match.
- You may call on your partner or either opponent.
4. **Respond** — The called player plays a wrestler face up into the ring, then calls on another player.
5. **Continue** — Repeat until all four players have played one wrestler.
6. **Resolve effects** — Wrestler effects trigger in the order they were played.
7. **Resolve the match** — Compare team totals. The winning team takes the Belt.
8. **Next match** — The player on the winning team who played their wrestler latest leads the next match.

### Resolving a Match

After all four wrestlers are in the ring and effects have been applied:

1. **Calculate base power** — Each team adds up the Power numbers of their two wrestlers.
2. **Apply type advantages** — Each wrestler checks the styles of both opposing wrestlers. For each opponent with a disadvantaged style, that wrestler gains +2 Power.
   - Clean beats Flashy (+2)
   - Flashy beats Dirty (+2)
   - Dirty beats Clean (+2)
3. **Apply Manager bonuses** — Add any bonuses from Manager abilities.
4. **Compare totals** — The team with the higher combined total power wins the match and takes the Belt.
5. **Ties** — If there's a tie, the team with the wrestler who has the highest individual Power wins. If still tied, it's a draw and nobody gets the Belt. Previous lead player leads the next match.


---

## Winning

After 6 matches, score the Belts. Unless otherwise specified, each Belt is worth 1 point. The team with the most points wins.

If it's tied, then it's a tie. You've met your match!

## Belts

### Backyard Brawler Belt
- All wrestlers with power 3 or less gain +1 power, but all wrestlers with power 4 or more lose 1 power.

### Bambino Belt
- The team with the lowest combined power wins this match.

### Bare Knuckle Belt
- Negate all effects from all wrestlers this match.

### Battle Royale Belt
- Call on players until everyone has played two wrestlers.

### Bicontinental "Boss" Belt
- Highest individual wrestler wins, not highest team total.

### Big Time Belt
- All wrestlers with power 5 or higher gain +1 power this match.

### Gambler's Belt
- All wrestlers are played face down and revealed simultaneously instead of using calling order.

### Interplanetary Belt
- Worth 2 points. The team that wins this match must discard one manager card.

### Super Championship Belt
- Worth 2 points. The player on the other team who played latest must lead the next mattch.
